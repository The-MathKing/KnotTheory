\textbf{Significance:} 
This is a phenomenal result. Furthermore, $|s(K)|$ consistently bounded $2g_4$, and $\tau$ consistently matched $g_4$. This cleanly severs any hypothesized linear transitivity between the signature and the $s$-invariant for positive knots.
