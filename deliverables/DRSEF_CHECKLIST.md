# DRSEF checklist — what to prepare and what to carry in

Fair: Beal Bank Dallas Regional Science and Engineering Fair, Fair Park.
Registration deadline **on or about 10 February 2027**, via **STEM Wizard**.
DRSEF follows **ISEF rules** in full; there is no separate DRSEF rulebook.

## Carry in on fair day — and nothing else

DRSEF permits only three things at the booth:

- [ ] **Display board** — `manuscript/zf_poster.pdf`
- [ ] **Printed official abstract** (the stamped/embossed copy)
- [ ] **Lab notebook / research binder**

No prototypes, no handouts, no extra supplies. A battery-operated personal
device is allowed only to show a prototype in action, which does not apply here.

## Display board

- Max **48 in wide × 30 in deep × 108 in tall**; table height 36 in.
  Current board is 48 × 48 in, which complies.
- **Removed** the sponsoring fair's name from the header — ISEF prohibits brand
  references and institutional crests on the display. Re-check before printing.
- Not permitted anywhere on the board: QR codes, URLs, email, phone, social
  media, handouts, commercial logos, published papers.
- The word "abstract" may not be used as a heading on the display; it applies
  only to the official stamped copy.
- Graphics need citations: own figures need software and year.

## Paperwork (STEM Wizard)

- [ ] Checklist Form 1
- [ ] Student Checklist Form 1A
- [ ] **Research Plan** — draft at `deliverables/research_plan.md`
- [ ] Approval Form 1B (adult sponsor)
- [ ] **Official Abstract, 250 words** — draft at `deliverables/abstract_250.md`
      (currently 245 words)

Forms 1C and 7 do not apply: no regulated research setting, no continuation.
The project has no human participants, vertebrate animals, hazardous biological
agents, chemicals or devices, so no SRC pre-approval beyond the basic forms
should be needed — confirm with the adult sponsor.

- [ ] **Virtual display board** — DRSEF requires one for judges to preview.
      The poster PDF serves; confirm the accepted format at registration.

## Research binder — assemble from what exists

- [ ] `manuscript/zf_paper.pdf` — the full paper (34 pp)
- [ ] `manuscript/zf_log.pdf` — the dated research log (22 pp). **Bring this.**
      It is the strongest artifact in the project: dated entries, refuted
      conjectures preserved with the wrong version intact, and a Status section
      listing eleven claims withdrawn or corrected. It is direct evidence for
      two rubric lines the poster cannot address — *degree of independence* and
      *understanding limitations*.
- [ ] `deliverables/presentation_pages/page-01..12.png` — the 12-page ISEF Project
      Presentation, one PNG per page, Mathematics/CS template order
- [ ] `results/zero_forcing/verify_all.txt` — 85 checks, all passing, one page
- [ ] Printout of `verification/certify_tiles.py` output for one tile, as a
      concrete example of what "certified" means

## Interview

25 of 100 points, against the poster's 10. Prep at
`deliverables/interview_prep.md`.
