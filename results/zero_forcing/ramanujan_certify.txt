Exact certification of the Ramanujan classification of P(n,k)
A cubic graph is Ramanujan iff every nontrivial eigenvalue has
|lambda| <= 2 sqrt 2; by Sunada this is the Riemann Hypothesis for
its Ihara zeta function.  Criterion used, exactly:
    D_k(u) = (7 + 4u T_k(u))^2 - 8(2u + 2T_k(u))^2  >=  0
at u = cos(2 pi j/n) for every nontrivial index j.
